## Production

Higgsfield Seed Audio supplied nine independent narration clips in the selected Grady voice. Narration generation used **23.10 Higgsfield credits**. The visuals use real app captures and native Higgsedit motion graphics. No generated footage was needed. Keeping the narration and chapters separate lets individual sections be revised independently.

The production archive includes narration, captions, source captures, the editable native project and composition scripts. The [spoken script](DEMO-SCRIPT.md) and [capture provenance](video-stills/README.md) document the presentation.

All ten MP4 files were decoded end to end without errors. [Export metadata and file hashes](video-verification.json) record their formats and durations.
