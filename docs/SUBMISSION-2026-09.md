Track: **AI in any startup**

## Judge walkthrough

Open the live app and select **FL-260901-A**. Explore four production batches, four customer destinations and 1,440 delivered packs. Select a batch to open its source record, search the document library, switch appearance, then save and read a trace report. The included bakery businesses and transactions are fictional sample records.

For document intake, use CSV or the connected Fireworks provider. Proposed records remain separate until approved. Original files and provider provenance stay available alongside the imported records. The OpenAI adapter remains configurable.
